There's a specific kind of town that only makes sense next to water — where the streets run toward the shoreline instead of away from it, where every business from the hardware store to the diner keeps half an eye on the tide chart, and where the off-season isn't an afterthought but its own entire personality. Those are the towns worth chasing, not the ones that are beachy for three months and forgettable for nine.

The best coastal small towns treat the water as infrastructure, not just scenery. Fishing fleets, boardwalks, ferry docks, surf breaks — the economy and the culture both run through the shoreline, which tends to produce towns with a stronger sense of self than a generic beach resort ever manages.

## What to look for

- **A working waterfront**, not just a beach. Fishing boats, marinas, and docks tend to signal a town that isn't only performing "coastal" for visitors.
- **An off-season you'd actually enjoy.** Ask what November looks like before you fall for a July postcard.
- **Storm and flood history.** Coastal charm comes with coastal risk — insurance costs and evacuation routes are worth knowing early.
- **Public shore access.** Some of the prettiest coastal towns have quietly privatized most of their beach. Check before you assume.

## Towns worth a look

<ul class="town-list">
  <li><strong>Rehoboth Beach, Delaware</strong> — A proper boardwalk town with a walkable, bookstore-and-bakery downtown a few blocks back from the sand.</li>
  <li><strong>Carmel-by-the-Sea, California</strong> — Fairy-tale cottages, cypress-lined streets, and a coastline dramatic enough that it's genuinely hard to look away from.</li>
  <li><strong>Kennebunkport, Maine</strong> — Working harbor, lobster shacks, and a New England charm that holds up even under a gray sky.</li>
  <li><strong>Tybee Island, Georgia</strong> — Low-key and unpretentious, a short drive from Savannah but with its own laid-back, barefoot identity.</li>
  <li><strong>Cannon Beach, Oregon</strong> — Dramatic sea stacks, cold water, and a foggy, moody coastline that trades tropical for genuinely beautiful.</li>
</ul>

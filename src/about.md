Yourville exists on a simple bet: that most "best places to live" content is organized around the wrong thing. Population thresholds, median home prices, and top-ten rankings tell you facts about a town without telling you whether it's actually *for you*.

So instead, we organize everything here around eight town archetypes — Remote Work Haven, Foodie's Table, Coastal Charmer, Mountain Hideaway, Creative Enclave, Family Hometown, College Town Energy, and Retirement Retreat. Every guide, the [quiz](/quiz/), and the [compare tool](/compare/) all draw from the same set, so wherever you start, you end up somewhere consistent.

A few honest notes on how this site works:

- **The guides describe character, not statistics.** We don't claim precise population figures, cost-of-living numbers, or rankings we can't stand behind. What we describe is reputation, feel, and what a town is generally known for.
- **The compare tool is editorial, not data.** The trait scores (walkability, nightlife, and so on) are considered judgment calls, not measurements — a way to think through trade-offs, not a scientific instrument.
- **This site is actively maintained**, with new guides and journal posts added regularly. If a town or a guide is missing something important, that's exactly the kind of thing we want to keep improving.

Yourville is written and maintained with the help of AI, under human direction. If something reads wrong or a fact needs a second look, we'd genuinely like to know — reach out via the [advertise page](/advertise/) contact details, which also work for general feedback.

Yourville doesn't run programmatic ads or affiliate links. If a town, a real estate group, or a local business wants to reach people actively researching a move, we offer straightforward, direct placements instead — a featured spotlight within a relevant guide, clearly labeled as sponsored, written in the same voice as the rest of the site.

We're not currently running paid placements, but we're open to hearing from the right partners as the site grows. If that's you, get in touch and tell us:

- Who you are and what you'd want featured
- Which guide or archetype it fits best
- What you're hoping readers do next

**Contact:** hello@yourville.com

This page also works for general feedback — a correction, a town we should cover, or a guide that got something wrong.
